> A curated list of tools, platforms, resources, communities, and guides for remote work.

Remote work has transformed how people build careers, run businesses, collaborate with teams, and live around the world. Whether working from home, a coworking space, or while traveling, remote professionals rely on technology, communication practices, productivity systems, and flexible work arrangements to succeed.

This list is intended for remote workers, distributed teams, freelancers, entrepreneurs, digital nomads, managers, and organizations building remote-first cultures.

## Remote Job Platforms

Platforms focused on remote and distributed work opportunities.

- [Remote OK](https://remoteok.com/) — Remote job board across technology and business roles.
- [We Work Remotely](https://weworkremotely.com/) — One of the largest remote job marketplaces.
- [FlexJobs](https://www.flexjobs.com/) — Curated remote and flexible job opportunities.
- [Wellfound](https://wellfound.com/) — Startup jobs with many remote opportunities.
- [Working Nomads](https://www.workingnomads.com/) — Remote jobs for digital professionals.
- [Remotive](https://remotive.com/) — Remote work community and job platform.
- [Jobspresso](https://jobspresso.co/) — Remote jobs in technology, marketing, and support.
- [Arc](https://arc.dev/) — Remote opportunities for developers and technical professionals.

## Communication & Collaboration

Tools that help distributed teams communicate effectively.

- [Slack](https://slack.com/) — Team messaging and collaboration platform.
- [Microsoft Teams](https://www.microsoft.com/microsoft-teams) — Workplace communication and collaboration suite.
- [Discord](https://discord.com/) — Community and team communication platform.
- [Mattermost](https://mattermost.com/) — Open-source team collaboration platform.
- [Google Workspace](https://workspace.google.com/) — Collaborative productivity suite.
- [Twist](https://twist.com/) — Asynchronous communication platform for remote teams.

## Productivity & Organization

Tools that help remote professionals stay productive.

- [Client Close Kit](https://darweesh128-cmd.github.io/client-close-kit/) — Free browser freelance invoice, timesheet, and expense log tools (no signup).
- [Todoist](https://todoist.com/) — Task management and personal productivity platform.
- [Motion](https://www.usemotion.com/) — AI-powered scheduling and planning assistant.
- [Clockify](https://clockify.me/) — Time tracking platform.
- [RescueTime](https://www.rescuetime.com/) — Productivity analytics and focus monitoring.
- [Forest](https://www.forestapp.cc/) — Focus enhancement application.
- [Toggl Track](https://toggl.com/track/) — Time tracking and reporting platform.
